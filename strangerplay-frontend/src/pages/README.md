## 💡 App Concept

- Two strangers connect via random video call
- They play funny games together live (Don't Laugh, Roast Battle, Word Sprint...)
- Crowd of other users watches & reacts
- Both players earn points per game (up to 100)
- At 100 pts → reward unlocked (to be designed)
- Funny moments auto-clipped → shared to socials = viral growth
